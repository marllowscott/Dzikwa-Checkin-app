# Pitch Deck Creation Plan for Dziwkwa Trust Check-In App

## Overview
Create a short, high-stakes pitch deck (5 slides max) to sell the Dziwkwa Check-In app to owners, emphasizing immediate ROI and payment. Use value-based selling to demonstrate how the app solves critical pain points and delivers measurable benefits. Focus on getting paid regardless of amount through compelling ROI metrics and urgency.

## Research Findings
- **Dziwkwa Trust**: Nonprofit organization founded in 2002 in Harare, Zimbabwe. Mission: Empower gifted orphans & vulnerable children through education scholarships, wellbeing support, performing arts, culture, sports, environmental awareness, and employability skills. Vision: Become the Charity of Choice by 2025. Supports ~400 children annually, with focus on empowering girls.
- **Brand Colors**: Blue-teal color scheme (primary colors in the app codebase indicate this palette).
- **App Functionality**: Comprehensive check-in system with admin dashboard, guest management, workshop tracking, stats/analytics, export capabilities, and real-time monitoring.

## Pain Points Identified
- Manual tracking of participants, visitors, and activities leading to inefficiencies and errors.
- Lack of real-time data for decision-making in education and wellbeing programs.
- Difficulty managing scholarships, community outreach, and multiple programs simultaneously.
- Inadequate analytics for measuring impact on vulnerable children and youth.
- Resource constraints in managing large-scale operations with limited staff (21 local staff + environmental team).

## App Solutions
- Automated check-in/out system reducing manual labor.
- Real-time analytics and reporting for better program management.
- Comprehensive guest/participant database with export capabilities.
- Workshop management and stats tracking for education programs.
- Admin controls for secure, efficient operations.
- Mobile-responsive design for field work and community outreach.
